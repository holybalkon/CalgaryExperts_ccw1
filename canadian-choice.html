Warranty

Check before you sign

  • Get the current written policy and the signed terms.
  • Confirm the exclusions, the service procedure and the transfer requirements.
  • Do not read the warranty as “everything covered” without checking the detailed conditions.

Official details: Canadian Choice warranty page.

Customer service

Editorial opinion, based on published feedback and not on mystery shopping: several Calgary testimonials describe polite communication, punctual arrivals, floor protection and cleanup after installation. Reports of service problems also exist, so the best-supported conclusion is that many customers report a well-managed installation, while response times and aftercare should be checked in recent third-party reviews.

What Calgary feedback mentions most

  • Courteous crews
  • On-time appointments
  • Clean work areas

Ask for in writing

  • A named service contact
  • The warranty response process
  • An itemized estimate that matches your window specification

We did not visit the company, mystery-shop its crews or measure response times.
