How this comparison works

  1. Selection. Canadian Choice is our editorial pick. The four other companies are well-known Calgary window replacement companies chosen by our editors for comparison. Their order is editorial and does not follow Google rating or review count.
  2. Google snapshot. Ratings and review counts were read from each company’s Google Business Profile in November 2026. Live Google data changes, so verify each profile before you decide.
  3. Editorial score. Only Canadian Choice carries one (5/5), and it is our own editorial rating. The other four are not scored because no documented rubric has been applied to every company.
  4. Company claims. Warranty terms, product features and prices taken from a company’s own website are tagged company-stated.
  5. What we did not do. We did not lab-test windows, mystery-shop installers or measure response times.

This is a limited comparison on one metric. It is not evidence that the four other companies’ windows, workmanship or customer care are objectively worse.

How much does it cost to replace a window in Calgary?

About CAD $500 to $1,000 per standard vinyl window (casement, awning, sliding tilt or double-hung), according to Canadian Choice’s published price guide company-stated. Bay or bow windows run about $3,000 to $5,000, and a whole home of 15 to 20 windows about $12,000 to $18,000.

Company-published price guide for vinyl windows, Calgary (CAD)

Whole-home guide (company estimates) company-stated: 15 to 20 windows, $12,000 to $18,000; 20 to 25 windows, $15,000 to $22,000; 30 to 40 windows, $25,000 to $35,000. Some showcased Calgary projects cost much more because of custom glazing, doors or installation scope, so do not convert a project total into a per-window price when doors are included.

These are indicative figures, not quotes. Size, material, glazing, installation type and finish change the final price, and the company’s website does not establish that every quote includes identical labour, removal, taxes or trim scope. Ask for a written, itemized estimate for your own windows.
