About this Calgary window replacement comparison

This page explains who publishes the comparison, how the companies were chosen, and what each number on the home page does and does not mean.

Publisher and disclosure

CalgaryExperts is an independent comparison desk in Calgary. Canadian Choice Windows & Doors, a Calgary window and door manufacturer and installer, is the company ranked first because it is our editorial pick.

This is a desk-research comparison, not an independent lab test, and nothing on the site claims independent testing. The editorial score is our own opinion and is always shown apart from public Google ratings.

How the companies were selected

Canadian Choice is our editorial pick. The four comparison companies, Weather Pro, Centra, Durabuilt and A-Apollo, were chosen by our editors as well-known Calgary window replacement companies. Their order is editorial and does not follow Google rating or review count.

This is a limited desk-research comparison. Being listed second to fifth is not evidence that the four comparison companies’ windows, workmanship or customer care are objectively worse than Canadian Choice’s, and a Google rating alone cannot show that either.

What the scores mean

The editorial score and the Google snapshot are always shown separately.

  • The editorial score of 5/5 for Canadian Choice is our own editorial rating, not a Google customer rating and not proof of market leadership.
  • The Google snapshot is the rating and review count read from each company’s Google Business Profile in November 2026: Canadian Choice 4.6 (1,468), Weather Pro 4.4 (354), Centra 4.9 (29), Durabuilt 4.4 (810) and A-Apollo 4.2 (299). Centra’s rating rests on few reviews and is less stable than the others.
  • Live Google data changes. The figures are not identical to those on other aggregators or business directories, so verify each profile immediately before you decide.

Sources and review policy

Product, warranty and price information comes from each company’s own website and is tagged company-stated wherever it has not been independently verified. Balanced context for Canadian Choice comes from its BBB Calgary profile and its Trustpilot page.

Individual customer reviews are only reproduced from original review pages, with attribution, date and permission. Where that has not been done, this site links to the source instead of quoting it.

We did not lab-test windows, mystery-shop installers, or measure response times. Performance depends on the exact model, so ask each company for ENERGY STAR and NFRC documentation.

Corrections and updates

Companies and readers can ask for a correction by calling the Canadian Choice Calgary showroom on 403-775-6198 or through its Calgary page. Please name the line concerned and a public source for the change.
